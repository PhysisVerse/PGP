# PGP76: Vested Allocation Renewal for DAO Founder Partner

- Author(s): [@arkitoure](https://github.com/arkitoure)
- Start Date: 2026-11-26
- Category: Economic
- Governance Role: Council
- Original PGP Pull Request:
- Tracking Issue:
- Vote Requirements: Council

---

## Summary

This proposal seeks Council approval to renew an existing vesting contract under the **Founder Partner** tier for a total of **800,000 PHY**. Any renewal incentive is included in that total.

---

## Impact

The renewal extends the holder’s vesting commitment for a further term. It applies only to the contract being renewed.

---

## Stakeholders

- **Founder Partner:** Elects to renew and agrees to the vesting terms.
- **DAO Council:** Reviews the terms and votes on the renewal.

---

## Explanation

For this renewal, the proposed classification uses the allocation’s reference value on 26 November 2026: **approximately 551.22 SOL**, above the **400 SOL** Founder Partner threshold. No new SOL contribution is involved.

---

## Pitfalls

Recording the full renewal as a new Treasury allocation would count the existing balance twice. DAO records should distinguish the PHY carried forward from any additional PHY awarded.

---

## Rationale

[PGP53](https://github.com/PhysisVerse/PGP/blob/main/0053_Vesting_Extension_Incentive_Reserve.md) provides the precedent for a holder to extend an existing vesting contract voluntarily. If the renewal does not proceed, the original terms continue to govern.

---

## Queries

Before the Council vote:

- Identify the original contract and confirm how much of the total is carried forward and how much, if any, is a renewal incentive.
- Confirm the effective date and vesting term, including any initial hold.

---

## Deployment

Following Council approval and holder acceptance, execute the renewal on-chain and link it to the original contract in the DAO’s vesting records.

---

## Metrics

Verify that the on-chain contract covers **800,000 PHY** and that its vesting dates match the terms approved by Council.
